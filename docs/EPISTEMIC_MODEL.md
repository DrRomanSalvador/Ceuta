# CeutIA — Epistemic Model

**Document class:** Normative epistemic contract
**Authority:** Binding on all agents, models and automated processes operating on CeutIA.

---

## 1. Core separation (non-negotiable)

| Concept | Definition | Forbidden conflation |
|---------|------------|----------------------|
| Observation | Raw recorded datum | Evidence |
| Evidence | Observation + provenance + context + capture method | Claim / Assertion |
| Assertion | Evaluable declarative statement | Demonstrated fact |
| Inference | Derived conclusion under explicit assumptions | Causality |
| Hypothesis | Provisional proposition under test | Calibrated prediction |
| Signal | Statistical or structural indicator | Operational risk |
| Prediction | Forward-looking estimate | Calibrated probability |
| Risk | Potential exposure under uncertainty | Certainty |

---

## 2. Absolute rules

1. Evidence confidence is never automatically converted into event probability.
2. A derivative source never counts as independent evidence.
3. Association never implies causation.
4. A proxy-gate pass never authorises operational promotion.
5. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.
6. Temporal leakage, dependent sources and contradictory sources must be explicitly classified and cannot be silently averaged.

---

## 3. Allowed epistemic states (closed set)

- `NO_VERIFICADO`
- `INSUFICIENTE`
- `PROXY_RISK`
- `CONSISTENTE`
- `CONTRADICTORIO`
- `DEPENDIENTE`
- `TEMPORAL_LEAKAGE`
- `BLOQUEADO`
- `VALIDADO` (only after reproducible, recorded verification)

Any other state label is unauthorised.
