## 3. Initial market thesis

**HYPOTHESIS H1:** The most capital-efficient first commercial path is likely not to sell the whole CeutIA/SERPIENTE platform. It is to productize a narrow, managed **evidence-backed operational risk intelligence service** for an organization with geographically distributed assets and a recurring need to detect, contextualize and act on external risks.

Candidate initial buyer family:
- corporate security;
- business continuity/resilience;
- operational risk;
- executive risk;
- crisis management;
- organizations with multi-site or cross-border operations.

The first product should initially avoid medical decision-making and other use cases that could unnecessarily create a high regulatory burden.

This is an ICP hypothesis requiring customer discovery.

## 13. Scientific consequences

Before marketing predictive performance:
- precommit target, horizon and evaluation window;
- define baseline comparators;
- preserve chronological separation;
- evaluate calibration and out-of-sample performance;
- test revision/future leakage;
- measure false-alert burden and decision usefulness;
- distinguish predictive validity from causal impact.

## 14. First experiments

E1 — 10 customer discovery interviews.
- Hypothesis: target teams experience a recurring, expensive monitoring/decision problem.
- Success: repeated evidence of a specific workflow failure plus identifiable budget owner.
- Falsification: problem is infrequent, low-cost or already solved satisfactorily.

E2 — 3 workflow demos using real or permissioned historical/public data.
- Hypothesis: the evidence-to-decision workflow is visibly better than the customer's current workflow.
- Success: customer requests a scoped pilot and agrees to commercial terms discussion.
- Falsification: demo is interesting but does not change a buying decision.

E3 — 1 paid pilot.
- Hypothesis: a customer will pay for the narrow service.
- Success: executed paid contract and agreed evaluation criteria.
- Falsification: repeated refusal to pay after qualified discovery.

E4 — renewal/expansion test.
- Hypothesis: delivered outcome is valuable enough to continue.
- Success: renewal or expansion based on measured value.
- Falsification: customer declines despite successful delivery because value is insufficient or procurement friction dominates.
