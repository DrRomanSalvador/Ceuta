## Discovery 2 incorporation

The denominator must be attached to the phenomenon and exposure definition, not assumed to be resident population. The existing `DynamicDenominator` integration is evidence of an engineering primitive, not proof that Ceuta's present-population/mobility denominator is empirically validated.

Required ESTRATEGA consequence: every actionable rate/risk interpretation must carry population scope, denominator identity, coverage and uncertainty. If the denominator is not fit for the phenomenon, actionability is restricted rather than silently transported.

## Discovery 3 incorporation

The preferred output under deep uncertainty is a state distribution plus plausible trajectories, change factors, reversible actions, monitoring indicators and withdrawal/escalation conditions. A single binary crisis state is insufficient where uncertainty materially changes the decision.

Required ESTRATEGA consequence: decision support must preserve scenario plurality and trigger/expiry semantics.

## Discovery 4 incorporation

An alert is not demonstrated useful merely because it was generated. The actionable chain must be observable as:

`ALERT -> RECEIPT -> INTERPRETATION -> DECISION -> ACTION -> OUTCOME`

Required ESTRATEGA consequence: alert utility must be measured at the decision boundary, including non-action and reasons for non-action.

## Discovery 5 incorporation

Complexity requires an incremental-value demonstration against a simpler baseline, outside sample and by relevant horizon/subgroup/regime/cost. ESTRATEGA must not request or justify complex models merely because they increase representational detail.
